# Article 5: Amendments

## 5.1 Process
1. Create a new article in `swarmforge/constitution/articles/` (e.g., `99_proposed_change.md`).
2. Route it to the **specifier** via `git_handoff` with priority `00`.
3. The **specifier** incorporates approved constitution changes into spec/prompt
   files; **QA** lands them on `main` after approval and the coordinator does
   backlog bookkeeping (BL-247).

## 5.2 Voting (if needed)
- For controversial changes, the **coordinator** may call a vote among roles.
- Majority approval is required.

## 5.3 A Consolidation Never Drops A Human Sentence
- **A consolidation never drops a human sentence.** When intakes or tickets are
  merged, split, or otherwise reshaped before entering the pipeline, every
  directive quoted from a human survives verbatim into the resulting ticket or
  tickets. A consolidation that cannot preserve one is refused rather than
  trimmed.
- The clause binds the ACT of consolidating, not any one office. It therefore
  reaches whoever consolidates next — including a consolidator this
  constitution has not met yet — which is why it is stated here as law rather
  than left in the contract of whichever office happens to hold the authority
  today.
- Ratified by explicit human decision 2026-07-26 under 5.1 (BL-681), separately
  from the consolidation mechanics it constrains (BL-680): a guarantee meant to
  outlive an office cannot be carried by that office's own contract.
