### New failure class: "landed but never handed off"

A parcel's git work merges cleanly into `main` (via a role's own merge-up
commit), but the *next* role never receives an actual mailbox parcel for
it, so the ticket sits stuck at its old stage indefinitely with no live
work anywhere. Found in two distinct flavors today, five instances total:

1. **QA approved and landed, but never notified the coordinator**:
   BL-1043, then BL-1083 and BL-1086 together. Each time,
   `build_freshness_cli sync` or a coder note surfaced the ticket sitting
   in `active/` with real, already-landed QA-approved work and no
   coordinator mailbox trail. Fixed each time by asking QA to resend the
   approval note, then bookkeeping normally once it arrived.
2. **Documenter passed to QA, but QA never received a parcel**: BL-1063
   and BL-1088. Both show a full
   coder→cleaner→architect→hardener→documenter chain in git history,
   landed on `main`, but no handoff ever reached QA's mailbox or QA's own
   worktree branch. Routed both by hand with a `git_handoff` citing the
   documenter-pass commit. (BL-1063's attempt correctly bounced itself — a
   live parcel already existed for it at architect from a bounce/refix
   cycle, so the ancestry read was one stage stale; no harm done, nothing
   double-dispatched.)

The specifier independently minted **BL-1100** for exactly this pattern
("QA-landed ticket never closed strands in active") — recurring enough now
(5 instances, 2 flavors) that it should stop being a per-instance
coordinator workaround and get a real fix. (Later in the shift, BL-1100
was re-specced by a second reviewer as "promotion candidacy is decided by
structured fields, never prose" — see below; both framings describe
symptoms of the same underlying prose-vs-structured-state gap.)

### Live bug in promotion tooling: a human-reserved ticket promoted three times

`promote_and_route_next.sh`'s auto-picker promoted **BL-1082** into an open
active slot despite an explicit human/Cursor directive on file twice today
("BL-1052/1053/1082 reserved for human/Cursor build, swarm must not
promote/dispatch... resume after 1078/1079/1080/1081 land"). The park was
recorded only as prose in the ticket's `notes:` field — no `depends_on`, no
`status: blocked` — which nothing in the promotion chokepoint reads.
Reverted it twice by hand; it came back a third time (evidently promoted by
concurrent activity on the shared checkout). **Root-caused and fixed**:
moved BL-1052, BL-1053, and BL-1082 into `backlog/hold/`, which every
promote-picker structurally excludes from its scan (Article 3.1) — the
first correct, tooling-respected home for a "reserved, do not
auto-promote" ticket, rather than a prose note in `paused/`. Confirmed
holding as of this writing.

**Same script's opposite bug, also found and worked around**: its
`is_do_not_promote()` check is a bare case-insensitive grep for the phrase
"do not promote" anywhere in a ticket file, and false-positived on
BL-1071, BL-1030, and BL-1031 — all contain that exact phrase in unrelated
prose describing something else entirely. All three are genuine, approved,
high-severity/expedite-eligible work; promoted manually (`git mv` +
`commit_integrity_cli` + `route_backlog_to_coder.sh <id>`) rather than
fighting the false refusal. Three independent false positives on the same
naive-grep pattern in one day — **BL-1100 was specced and approved to fix
this at the root** (promotion candidacy decided by structured fields, never
prose); not yet built.
