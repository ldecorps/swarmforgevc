# Let's Talk more chiptunes (BL-705)

Hold music during Let's Talk **thinking** now includes short homage loops
for Thanatos, Ghost'n Goblins, Zelda Overworld, Tron Lightcycle, plus
Tetris and Mega Man — alongside the existing Zappa set.

## What changed

- Same quiet Web Audio step player (no YM file decoder).
- Title line still shows the chosen song name.
- Picker still avoids repeating the same song immediately.
- Android float companion hold music catalog mirrors the Mini App list.

## How to hear them

1. Open Let's Talk (Mini App or Android bubble panel).
2. Leave **Hold music** on.
3. Send a turn and wait in **thinking** — a random catalog track plays quietly.
4. Toggle Hold music off or **Pause all** to silence it.

## Out of scope

Atari ST YM loader / licensed soundtrack dumps — deferred.
