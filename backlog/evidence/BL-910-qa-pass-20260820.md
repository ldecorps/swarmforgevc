## G5 — a correction worth recording

My first probe appeared to show the implementation disagreeing with a hand calculation at `openN=21, net 0.7/d` (code said 30 days, my check said 31). **The code was right and my probe was wrong.** In IEEE floats `21 / 0.7 = 30.000000000000004`, which `Math.ceil` lifts to 31; exact arithmetic gives `210 / 7 = 30`. That float dust is precisely what `projectNotDoneEta`'s integer-tenths division exists to eliminate, and a human dividing the two numbers printed on the chart gets 30. The 106,140-case sweep in G5 was then run against exact integer arithmetic rather than float, and found zero disagreements.
