case 03 invoked `swarm_handoff.bb` once under `SWARMFORGE_SKIP_DAEMON=1`
and asserted exit 0. The first invocation of any `git_handoff` draft is
the self-audit challenge (Article 2.3): it prints `AUDIT_REQUIRED` /

passed without ever reaching delivery — vacuous from birth (2026-08-30).
