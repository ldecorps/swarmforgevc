## What changed (user/operator-visible)

BL-695 (2026-08-25) correctly restricted git-tracked
`backlog/topics/*.json` records to `ticket` ids, but its marker-store
change went further: `recordSwarmIconId` had a durable store only for
`ticket` and `supervisor` kinds, so a marker write for `epic`/`standing`/
`role` ids (classify `unbound`) silently wrote nothing while
`syncTopicIcon` still reported `updated` — inert for 3 of 4 topic kinds
since the regression landed, 5 tests red the whole time. Fixed with a
second untracked store, `.swarmforge/topic-icons.json`
(`topicThreadKind.ts`), shared by epic/standing/role ids; supervisor keeps
its existing separate file. `recordSwarmIconId` now returns `'recorded' |
'refused'` explicitly; `syncTopicIcon` gains `'icon-set-marker-unrecorded'`
for anything not `'recorded'`, replacing the false `'updated'`.
