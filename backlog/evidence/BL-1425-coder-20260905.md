# BL-1425 — coder pass, 2026-09-05

## What was wanted

Human directive, verbatim: "ammend qjump behaviour: it should.place the
ticket on rails, ignoring the max cap". BL-1083 (2026-08-23) routed every
promotion through `promotion_gates_lib.bb`'s chokepoint, whose chain ends
in `depth-refusal`, so a full active_backlog_max_depth cap refused the
Approvals Q jump tap, the `/qjump` verb, and the paused-pager Expedite -
the ticket stayed paused with its approval recorded. This directive
reverses that one row: a queue-jump now crosses the depth cap only,
telling the operator it did.

## What landed

- **`swarmforge/scripts/promotion_gates_lib.bb`**: `depth-refusal` split
  into a shared pure predicate `depth-exceeded?` (used by both
  `depth-refusal` and the new crossing check, so the "is the cap
  exceeded" condition is stated once) and the refusal-map builder itself
  (behavior unchanged). `evaluate` gained `:queue-jump?` - when true and
  the cap would have refused, the depth gate is skipped and the result
  carries `{:crossed {:gate "active_backlog_max_depth" :reason "active
  count N >= cap M"}}` alongside `:ok true`; every other gate (hold, epic,
  blocked, human_approval, acceptance, slice_size_envelope, depends_on)
  runs in the exact same order, unaffected. New `crossed-advisory-line`
  formats the stderr signal.
- **`swarmforge/scripts/promotion_gates_cli.bb`**: `gate-promotion` takes
  a trailing `--queue-jump` flag (the literal fixed at mint), passes
  `:queue-jump? true` to `evaluate`, and prints
  `ADVISORY|active_backlog_max_depth|queue-jump past cap: active count N
  >= cap M` to stderr when the result carries `:crossed` - stdout's
  `ALLOW|<file>` contract is byte-identical to before.
- **`extension/src/panel/backlogWriter.ts`**: `promoteToActive` takes
  `{queueJump?: boolean}`, forwarded to the CLI. `runGateCli` switched from
  `execFileSync` to `spawnSync` (the only way to capture stderr on the
  ALLOW/exit-0 path, where the crossed advisory now lives).
  `parseGateVerdict` reads a `crossed` outcome **gate-name-agnostically**
  (the ADVISORY line that is not `orthogonality` - BL-854's own advisory -
  is the crossing signal), so `active_backlog_max_depth` is never restated
  as live code outside the chokepoint (BL-1083 invariant 1, still
  enforced by its own property test).
- **`extension/src/tools/telegramFrontDeskBotCore.ts`**:
  `recordExpediteDecisionAndClose` - the routine BOTH the Q jump button
  tap and the `/qjump` verb reply share (BL-721) - always requests
  `{queueJump: true}` (this routine IS the queue-jump path by
  construction) and, on a crossing, notifies the Approvals topic once
  (`${backlogId}: Q jumped past the active_backlog_max_depth cap: ...`) -
  centralized here rather than duplicated in each of the two callers.
  `PromotionOutcome` gained `crossed?`.
- **`extension/src/concierge/expediteSafety.ts`**: new
  `crossedCapToastText(reason)`, alongside its sibling
  `unsafeDispatchToastText`. The Q jump button tap's own
  `answerCallbackQuery` toast also reads it directly (an additional,
  immediate signal on that channel).
- **`extension/src/bridge/bridgeServer.ts`**: the paused-pager Expedite
  route passes `{queueJump: true}` and its 200 body carries `crossed` when
  present.
- **`specs/pipeline/steps/bl1425QueueJumpPastCapSteps.js`** (new): drives
  the real `promoteToActive` (shelling to the real
  `promotion_gates_cli.bb`) for scenario 01, and the real
  `recordExpediteDecisionAndClose` with a pure stub `promoteTicketIfPaused`
  for scenario 02 (per the ticket's own direction - no fixture root
  needed there).

## Declared invariants

1. **"Only a caller-declared queue-jump crosses the depth cap... no script
   the coordinator or the daemon runs ever declares the mode"** - the
   flag is a literal (`--queue-jump` / `queueJump: true`), never derived
   from a ticket field; acceptance scenario 03 reads
   `promote_and_route_next.sh`, `route_backlog_to_coder.sh`,
   `handoffd.bb`, `chase_sweep_lib.bb` and confirms none declares it
   (`grep -c -- --queue-jump` on all four: 0). Scenario 01's rows 3-5
   confirm hold/depends_on/blocked still refuse a queue-jump exactly as
   any promotion.
2. **"The bypass lives inside the one promotion-gates chokepoint... no
   second copy of the depth-cap rule exists in TypeScript"** -
   `depth-exceeded?`/`depth-refusal` stay the sole source; TypeScript's
   crossed-parsing is gate-name-agnostic (matches any non-orthogonality
   ADVISORY line), verified by BL-1083's own still-passing property test
   (P2: no gate rule name appears as live code outside the chokepoint).
3. **"A queue-jump past the cap is never silent and never widens the
   cap"** - the Approvals topic, the button toast, and the pager's 200
   body all carry the crossed signal (scenario 02); the ticket fills a
   real active slot (no cap-widening logic anywhere), confirmed by
   scenario 01 row 1 landing the candidate in `active`.
