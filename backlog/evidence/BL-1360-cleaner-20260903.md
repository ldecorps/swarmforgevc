over the 100-site threshold. Weighed against a split and declined:

- It is one feature's step-handler file, the established one-file-per-feature
  convention in `specs/pipeline/steps/` (sibling files in this directory run
  500-1000+ lines; this is smaller than all of them).
- Its sections (Background/Given/When/Then) are already the natural seams;
  splitting by section would separate assertions that share one `ctx.bl1360`
  fixture and one `run()` helper for no structural gain — a mechanical chop,
  not a cohesion improvement.

  files with no corresponding scenario-file split, which the codebase does
  not do elsewhere.

Kept whole per the ticket's own guidance: "A legitimately-cohesive large
module that a split would only harm stays whole."
