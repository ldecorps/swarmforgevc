## Scope reading — confirmed, not a defect

Both the coder and cleaner flagged (and I independently confirm): the waive
silences only the coordinator NUDGE. The same finding's operator
`BABYSITTER_ESCALATION` (`decide-escalations` is still called with the
unfiltered `findings`, not the waive-filtered `nudgeable`) will continue to
fire every cooldown for a waived CRIT finding. This is NOT a defect against
what was asked: the ticket's `invariants`, `description`'s HOW section, and
the acceptance feature itself all speak exclusively of "nudge" — never
"escalation" — and the ticket's own `constraints` reserve generalizing the
mechanism as an explicit follow-up ("If the mechanism generalizes, that is a
follow-up with its own evidence"). Implementing escalation-suppression here
would have been scope creep beyond what was reviewed and approved. Recording
this here (as the coder and cleaner did) so a future ticket generalizing the
waive to escalation has a clear starting reference, not because this parcel
owes it.
