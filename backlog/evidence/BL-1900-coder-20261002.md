## Should queue entries expire once their land is on origin/main?
Not by land alone. A landed replay on origin/main is approved only through its source (BL-1334
mapping), and the source's approval now comes from its queue entry. Expire the entry when the land
reaches origin/main, and that replay reads unapproved again on every later scan of main's
history (the babysitter's Article 4.2 sweep, check_pipeline_code_on_main.sh): the 2026-10-02
outage again. Cost is no reason to expire either: 0.09 s rev-list plus a grep over ~17k lines at
12 entries. If it ever needs bounding, the safe move is to retire an entry only once nothing
reads its replay any more (for example, its land record is older than the sweep's window). That
would be its own ticket; nothing is changed here.
