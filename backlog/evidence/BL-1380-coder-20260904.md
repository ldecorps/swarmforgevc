# BL-1380 — CODER PASS, 2026-09-04

## The defect, confirmed at the source before anything changed

`handlePausedPagerExpediteRoute` called `recordApprovalReply(targetPath,
backlogId)` with no ruling argument. That signature takes the
`approveHumanApprovalText` branch, which flips `human_approval` and never
writes `human_ruling:` — so a ticket declaring `ruling_options` was left
approved with its choice discarded, the ask no longer pending, and no way to
ask again. The ticket's own reproduction notes hold exactly as written; nothing
moved.

The human's ruling (recorded on the ticket, 2026-09-04) is option 1: refuse
with a 409 naming the gate and the ruling options.

## What changed

`extension/src/bridge/bridgeServer.ts`

- `classifyExpediteRulingRefusal` (new): asks BL-1367's
  `classifyApprovalRulingRequirement` whether this ticket poses a choice nobody
  has answered, and returns the 409 body if so. The route calls it BEFORE any
  write, as the Approve route does.
- The route's `recordApprovalReply` call is now reached only past that gate.

**One deliberate difference from the Approve route, and why.** Approve
validates a label the SURFACE just offered; Expedite offers none, so what is
weighed is the ruling already ON RECORD (`readRecordedRuling`). Only
`ruling-required` refuses. `unknown-option` here would mean a human answered
through the ruling keyboard in words that no longer match a declared label —
an answer that exists, and not this route's to re-judge. Refusing it would
leave the verb dead on tickets the operator HAS answered, which is precisely
the over-correction BL-1083 warns against. A ticket declaring no options
reaches `ok` and expedites byte-for-byte as today (invariant 3).

No second copy of the rules was written: the classifier is reused, per the
ticket's direction.
