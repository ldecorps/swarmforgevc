# BL-1798 — coder pass, 2026-09-29

## What landed

A new `:local-compact` `:bootstrap-text-style` in `prompt_engine_lib.bb`,
applied to `local-model`'s own `provider-capabilities` entry only (was
`:generic`). `compose`'s dispatch (`case style`) gets one new branch:

```
:local-compact
(if (local-model-has-role-card? role)
  (local-compact-bootstrap-text role two-pack? overlay? overlay-prompt fragment-cache-atom fragment-content-fn)
  (generic-bootstrap-text role draft two-pack? overlay? overlay-prompt fragment-cache-atom fragment-content-fn))
```

`local-model-has-role-card?` is a real filesystem check
(`swarmforge/roles/local-model/<role>.note` exists) — the ONE decision
point that picks a whole shape, never a partial mix (invariant 2).
`local-compact-bootstrap-text` reads the shared loop card
(`swarmforge/roles/local-model/loop.note`) then the role's own card
through `read-fragment!`, exactly like the generic builder's `"role"`
fragment — the same BL-574 content-hash cache applies. A two-pack/overlay
flag appears only as a one-line pointer to the overlay file (What is
wanted item 5), never inlined.

Two new fragment names (`"local-loop"`, `"local-role-card"`) registered in
`fragment-names` and resolved by `fragment-source-path`.

Two new prose files, written per the ticket's own guidance (no Article or
role-prompt text restated, both point at where the full text lives):

| file | chars |
|---|---|
| `swarmforge/roles/local-model/loop.note` | 1,729 |
| `swarmforge/roles/local-model/coder.note` | 1,505 |

Measured (2026-09-29, this parcel): `local-model`/`coder` composes to
**3,235 characters** (well under the 8,192 budget — about a quarter of it,
matching the ticket's own "room to spare" framing), down from the 58,371
the mint measured for `claude`/`coder` (unchanged — see below). Every
other role composing through `local-model` with no card of its own falls
back to the generic composition byte-for-byte (verified: `operator`).

## Declared invariants (BL-654)

- **Invariant 1** ("Every agent other than local-model composes byte-
  identically to before this change, for every role, pack flag and
  overlay.") — **P1**
  (`bl1798_local_model_compact_card_property_runner.bb`): generates a
  (role, agent≠local-model, two-pack?, overlay-prompt) request and asserts
  (a) that agent's capabilities entry never reports `:local-compact`, (b)
  `compose`'s own `:system-prompt` equals an INDEPENDENTLY reconstructed
  reference built by dispatching on that same style through a SEPARATE
  `case` in the test file (never local-compact's own branch), and (c) the
  composed text never contains a path unique to the local-model cards.
  Non-vacuous: broken by temporarily widening `codex`'s own capabilities
  entry to `:local-compact` — failed on every codex-generated case, both
  on the capabilities assertion and the reference-text comparison; restored
  and reconfirmed clean.
- **Invariant 2** ("A local-model seat's composed prompt is either the
  loop card plus its role's card within 8192 characters, or, for a role
  with no card, exactly today's generic composition — never a truncated or
  partial mix of the two.") — **P2**: generates a (role, two-pack?,
  overlay-prompt) request over a MIX of roles that do and do not have
  their own `.note`, and asserts EXACTLY one of two whole shapes: the
  full, untruncated loop-note content immediately followed by the full,
  untruncated role-note content (both read fresh from disk, independent of
  compose's own cache) as a PREFIX of the composed text with total length
  ≤ 8192; or the composed text is byte-identical to the generic (claude)
  composition of the same role/two-pack?/overlay-prompt. Non-vacuous:
  broken by truncating the role card to its first 10 characters inside
  `local-compact-bootstrap-text` — failed on every has-card
  (`role=coder`) case with the exact untruncated-prefix message, every
  no-card case stayed unaffected (confirming the mutant is isolated to the
  has-card branch, not a broader break); restored and reconfirmed clean.

**Reachability**: 60 runs each. P1: 9/9 distinct non-local-model agents
drawn (floor 4). P2: 10 has-card cases, 50 no-card cases (floor 6 each) —
both branches, including the has-card one the acceptance feature alone
would under-sample, well exercised.
