## What changed

One shared composer, `humanDecisionCommitMessage()` + `HUMAN_DECISION_BYLINE`
(`extension/src/util/commitIntegrityRunner.ts` — the module every human-decision
writer already imports), and three call sites now compose from it:

- `extension/src/bridge/bridgeServer.ts` — the paused-pager Approve route.
- `extension/src/tools/telegramFrontDeskBotCore.ts` — the bot's shared
  Approve/Reject/Amend template (and the ruling reply, which routes through it).
- `extension/src/tools/telegram-front-desk-bot.ts` — `commitExpediteWrites`.

The byline is `By the human, recorded by the front desk.` It names the decider
and no pipeline role, satisfying invariant 1.

**Scope note — a third site the ticket names only in passing.** The ticket lists
two writers; Expedite is a third that also records `human_approval` (its message
is `Expedite <id>: record approval + promotion`). Leaving it on `By coder.`
would be exactly the "surviving half" the ticket warns about, so it composes the
same byline. The other automated commits in `bridgeServer.ts` (epic reorder,
topic priority, BL topic record) are NOT human_approval records and were left
alone — out of this slice.
