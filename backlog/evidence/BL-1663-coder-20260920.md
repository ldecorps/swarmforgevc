## The fix

`extension/test/bl1474ReplayCommitRefusalReasonInvariants.property.test.js`
spawned a fresh `bb` process and loaded the whole `land_step_lib.bb` for
every fast-check draw (40 process starts per test), costing 6.6-8.2s alone
and timing out the property lane's 20000ms `testTimeout` under fork
contention.

Replaced the per-draw `replayCommitRefusalReason()` (one `spawnSync` per
call) with `replayCommitRefusalReasons(draws)`: `fc.sample(inputArb, 40)`
draws the same shapes as before, then a single `spawnSync('bb', ...)` maps
`land-step-lib/replay-commit-refusal-reason` over every draw in one bb
process and returns the array of reasons. Assertions and the BL-654
reachability floor are unchanged, evaluated per draw in JS against the
returned array. Still drives the real library function through bb - no
JS reimplementation of the branching.
