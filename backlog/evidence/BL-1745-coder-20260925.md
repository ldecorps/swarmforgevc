## The fix

Three subprocess-heavy property invariants declared no timeout of their
own, so they ran under the property lane's 20s default and timed out
under load contention:

- `extension/test/bl1670LandStrayConflictSupersededInvariant.property.test.js`
  (the whole file's one test).
- `extension/test/bl1108CursorSeatReadiness.property.test.js` invariant 1
  ("every configured agent token uses its own process marker") — invariant
  2 was not red and is untouched.

  the reverse-hop invariant — the other two tests in the file (the
  git_handoff-refused invariant and the non-vacuity floor) were not red
  and are untouched.

Direction chosen: declaring `SUBPROCESS_HEAVY_TIMEOUT_MS` (240000ms,
`extension/test/helpers/subprocessHeavyTimeout.js`, BL-932's shared
constant — bl1713 and others already import it) as the third argument to
each affected `test(...)` call, rather than BL-1725/BL-1663's
shared-fixture-plus-one-bb-process rewrite. The ticket offered this as an
explicit, equally valid option ("direction, not mandate"); it is the
minimal change that satisfies "the assertions, the three shapes and
`assertReachFloor` stay as they are" — none of the three files' test
bodies changed at all, only their timeout.
