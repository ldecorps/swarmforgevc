assumed from the coder's evidence:

git-ref comparison that only works transiently, pre-commit — not in whether
the widening was avoided (it was).

literal in the step or the ticket's own evidence file) rather than from

Alternatively, drop the git-diff self-check and let the property's own
runtime (now ~2.3s vs. the previous ~62s, an order of magnitude) stand as the
evidence, recorded in prose in the commit message as already done.
