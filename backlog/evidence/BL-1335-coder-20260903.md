## Built to the human's ruling

*"Promote automatically only when the classification is unambiguous, otherwise
announce for operator confirmation."* So `classify-evidence` has **three**
answers, and the default of the three is to do nothing:

| classification | what happens |
|---|---|
| unambiguous exhaustion | a failover record is opened |
| suspected (rate limit, 429, "usage limit") | **announced only** — the operator confirms |
| anything else, including unrecognised text | nothing at all |

The `:else` branch is `nil` by construction, so an unrecognised pane line fails
CLOSED rather than defaulting to exhaustion — the ticket's own constraint, and
the right posture when the input is pane text and the cost of a false positive
is a real seat swap.

## No third store, no second path (invariant 2)

`promotion-decision` builds the record in the shape
`provider_outage_record_lib/normalize-record` already expects and
`promote-exhaustion-to-failover!` appends it to
`outage-failover-store/outages-path` — **the file BL-669 already reads**. The
consumer needs no change at all, and nothing here forks a second assignment
path (BL-1178).

## The wiring anchor, where the ticket put it

`handoffd.bb::promote-exhaustion-to-failover!` is **called from the live tick**,
inside `observe-pane-provider-outage!` — the same pane snapshot that produces
the evidence also asks whether it should open a record. No second tmux capture
and no separate scanner over the evidence file. That is the anchor shape the
ticket named as the reason BL-840's producer is live today.

It never throws: a promotion that cannot be written must not take the pane
observation down with it.

## A defect my own property test found, before any reviewer saw it

The first `out of tokens` pattern demanded `(this|the) (week|month|period)`
exactly, and my generated corpus produced *"out of tokens until the next
week"* — real phrasing the pattern missed, which would have silently declined
to promote a genuine exhaustion. Widened to `\bout of (credits|tokens)\b`,
which is unambiguous however the period is worded. Recorded rather than quietly
corrected: the generator caught a real gap in the shipped classifier, which is
what it is for.
