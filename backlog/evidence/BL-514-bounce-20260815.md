Per the constitution's workflow rule "A Bounce Must Be Reverted Out Of The
Bouncing Branch" (BL-490/BL-495): bouncing sends a `note` back but does
**not** remove the bounced commit from the bouncer's own branch — the
bouncer must revert it in the same step as the send-back, unless the
bounced commit is already an ancestor of `main` (it is not here — `git
merge-base --is-ancestor fe2e4b8ad5 origin/main` returns false). No revert
commit exists anywhere between the bounce (`9ce82ec95`/`1771ff9cf`) and the
architect's subsequent approval-and-forward of BL-514
(`git log --oneline 9ce82ec95..d840300c0` shows none), and no coder rework
of `BridgeClient.kt` has landed since (`git log 9ce82ec95..ede6574276 --
android/.../BridgeClient.kt` is empty).

Because BL-514 and BL-765 share one coder branch (mono-router, single
resident), the un-reverted BL-765 commits are unavoidably ancestors of the
commit carrying BL-514's approved work. **Approving BL-514 as-is means QA
lands BL-765's known-defective, un-reviewed-by-QA code onto `main` under
BL-514's approval** — exactly the failure "An Approval Authorizes Only Its
Ticket's Work" (BL-506) and the bounce-revert rule exist to prevent. BL-765
itself is still `status: todo`, `bounce_count: 1`, active and not
abandoned — its fix belongs on `main` only after its own QA pass, not as a
rider on a sibling ticket's approval.

**Expected:** the branch QA is asked to approve for BL-514 contains only
BL-514's own work (plus whatever else has already independently cleared
QA), with any bounced-and-unreverted sibling work either fixed or reverted

remain un-reverted ancestors of the commit QA is asked to land, carrying
