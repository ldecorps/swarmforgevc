## What changed

Human ruling A (one chokepoint, recorded 84067ea1ce): `sh!`
(`daemon_cycle_guard_lib.bb`) gains a per-call `:bound-ms` override and a
`:kill-mode` option (`:tree` default, unchanged; `:group` — setsid +
`kill -KILL -- -<pgid>` before `destroyForcibly`, for the one caller that
needs the stricter whole-process-group kill). `bounded_run_lib.bb`'s
`run-bounded!` is now a thin wrapper over `daemon-cycle-guard-lib/sh!`
(`:bound-ms timeout-ms :kill-mode :group`), keeping its own signature and
`{:exit :timed-out?}` result shape (`:exit` on a bound hit is now `124`,
the chokepoint's own convention, instead of the old `nil` — callers branch
on `:timed-out?`, not the numeric value, in the timeout path; the one
place that asserted `nil` is `bounded_run_lib_test_runner.bb`, updated).
It no longer names `babashka.process` at all — the setsid wrap is pure
data (`(concat ["setsid"] cmd)`), and the group-kill lives in the
chokepoint's `destroy-timed-out-process!`.

The three plain waits route through `daemon-cycle-guard-lib/sh!`, same
call shapes:
- `ticket_close_guard_lib.bb`'s `ancestor-of-main?` (two `process/sh` calls
  at the ask/resolvable? closures).
- `unregistered_test_gate_lib.bb`'s private `git!`.
- `expedite_cli.bb`'s private `sh` helper (every one of its ~15 call
  sites changes nothing at the call site).

Each of the three now self-load-files `daemon_cycle_guard_lib.bb` rather
than relying on a loader (swarm_handoff.bb, task_scope_gate_lib.bb) to
have brought it in first — matches the existing convention every other
daemon-reachable lib follows.
