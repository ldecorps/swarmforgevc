# BL-1418 — coder pass, 2026-09-05

## What was wanted

Article 1.10 introduces the Art Director, but prose alone is not a seat:
`swarm_handoff.sh` refuses a recipient not in `.swarmforge/roles.tsv`,
`ensureRoleTopics` creates no topic for a role the icon map does not know,
the model factory cannot assign it a model, and `prepare_worktrees`
creates no worktree for a role the pack does not declare. Human ruling:
Standing pane (option 1) - an always-on window like every other role, its
own Telegram topic, reviews on wake.

## Regressions found and fixed (collateral: pre-existing "8 roles" assumptions)

`ALL_SWARM_ROLES` growing from 8 to 9 members broke three pre-existing
tests that hardcoded the old count/list, plus one property test whose own
generator now included art-director as if it were a real board column:
- `extension/test/roleTopicMapStore.test.js`: exact-array assertion
  updated to the 9-member list.
- `extension/test/telegramFrontDeskBotCli.test.js`: `calls.length === 8`
  updated to 9.
- `extension/test/bl1040SeatIdentityObservationPath.property.test.js`:
  `BOARD_STAGES` (already excluded `'coordinator'` for the identical
  reason) now also excludes `'art-director'`.
- `extension/src/concierge/pipelineBoard.ts`: the coordinator-only remap
  (`rawRole === 'coordinator' ? 'QA' : rawRole`) extended to art-director
  - caught by `pipelineBoard.property.test.js`'s own "every ALL_SWARM_ROLES
    role renders on a real column" property, which is otherwise falsified
    by any role added to the roster with no board column.
