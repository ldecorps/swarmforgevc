`"ready_for_next.sh"`, keeping `git commit`/`merge`/`checkout`/`switch`/
`restore`/`reset`/`rebase`/`cherry-pick`/`revert`/`stash`,

`local_model_repeat_guard.bb:674-688`, wired at line 802).
