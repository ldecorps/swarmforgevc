## Invariant (BL-654)

Declared invariant: "A reach floor is met by construction: every value a
floor names is produced by a draw plan that yields it on every run, never
by a uniform draw the floor merely hopes will cover it."

Coder-authored `draw_schedule_lib_property_runner.bb` (300 runs default,
`PROPERTY_RUNS` overrides): for `runs` drawn uniformly from 9..48 and 300
distinct seeds, asserts `seat-schedule` returns exactly `runs` plans and
that two-seat/three-seat/all-busy coverage meets 6/3/4 on **every** draw
(never a sampled pass rate), plus every plan's `n-seats` is 2 or 3 and
`n-parcels` is in `1..n-seats+1`. No `*.property.test.js` home exists for
Babashka (BL-472 deferred); follows this repo's established bb-lane
precedent instead (`ambulance_lib_property_runner.bb`,
`expedite_lib_property_runner.bb`) — noted in the file's own header.

Non-vacuity: temporarily raised the lib's `runs >= 9` branch threshold to
`999999` (forcing every draw through the uniform-only branch regardless of
`runs`) → 29/300 property failures reported (`floor(s) missed by
construction`) → reverted; re-ran green.
