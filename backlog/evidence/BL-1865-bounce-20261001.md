## D1 — scenario 01's bound was a projection, never a measurement (spec-gap, blamed: specifier)

At mint, scenario 01 required each test to finish alone in under 8000 ms
in its fastest of three runs. The bound assumed that once the lib loaded
only once, the cost left per draw would fit 296 ms (8000 / 27). Nobody had
measured a fixed build, so the bound broke the BL-1658 D2 rule: a budget
comes from a quiet-host measurement with stated headroom.

The coder built the remedy as directed. The lib now loads once per test,
the 27 draws and the assertions are unchanged, and
`propertyLaneTimeoutMs(20000)` is unchanged. The coder then measured the
fixed build at about 340-530 ms a draw. That cost is buildRepo's git
spawns plus `land-plan`/`own-paths`'s own git calls, and invariant 2
requires both fresh on every draw. The fastest of three for invariant 1
was 7840, 12694, 11387 and 9320 ms over four batches, at 1-minute loads of
1.8-3.0. That is 40-63% of the 20000 ms base, down from 92% at mint.
