  private). There is no other change to the land step.
- `land_merge_path_lib.bb` (pure):

    the landing ticket and the open ticket ids. It returns, per registry, what the land step's
    pass would change: `rows-to-retire` over the tree's rows and `rows-to-restore` against

  - `registry-decline-reason` turns those changes into the fallback reason.

    separately.

  with `qa-hold-lib/open-ticket-ids-for`. A non-empty change declines to the land step (`LAND_PATH
  land-step: the land step's registry pass would change ...`) with nothing pushed. An unreadable
  register also declines (fails closed). The check runs again on the race-retry rebuild.
