## A real gap found: picking the most recent `sent-at` relied on insertion order, not value

`findPreviousBriefingSentAtIso`'s new loop tracks the best (latest, still
before `now`) `sent-at` entry via `if (ms > bestMs)`. Hand-mutating this
to `if (true)` (always overwrite) survived against the full 38-test
suite unchanged — the one existing multi-entry test
("picks the MOST RECENT sent-at... across several recorded sends")
happened to insert its map entries in chronological order, so
`Object.values`' insertion-order iteration coincidentally matched the
correct chronological answer regardless of whether the comparison ran at
all. Added "picks the most recent sent-at by VALUE, not by insertion
order in the map" — the same two dates, inserted in the REVERSE order —
confirmed to fail against the `if (true)` mutant and pass against the
real code.

The sibling mutant (`>` weakened to `>=`) was checked and confirmed
equivalent, not missed: a tie (`ms === bestMs`) can only arise from two
entries recording the literal same instant, and the one writer of this
map (`briefing_email_lib.bb`'s `record-briefing-sent!`, fed by
`(str (Instant/now))`) always serializes an instant to Java's canonical
`Instant.toString()` format — so two entries with identical `ms` are
necessarily byte-identical strings too, making `>` vs `>=` unobservable
by construction, not merely untested. Grep-verified: `(str
(Instant/now))` is the only writer this field has.
