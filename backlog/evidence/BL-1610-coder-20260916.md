# BL-1610 — coder, 2026-09-16

## What was wrong

BL-1576's merge-drop send gate bounds its merge scan to
`received..forwarded`. When the received parcel is the coordinator's
route git_handoff (commit = main's own tip at promotion), that range is
the sender's ENTIRE off-main history — 66 merges on the coder branch on
2026-09-16. The coder's BL-1606 send was refused over nine findings on
six merges from hours earlier, all cross-role merges whose one-sided
resolutions later merges had already superseded — on paths the forward
carried no version of at all (every implicated path's blob at the
forwarded commit equalled its blob at the received commit).

## The fix

- `ready_for_next_task.bb` / `ready_for_next_batch.bb`: stamp
  `received_at_head` (the sender's own HEAD, 10-hex) onto the in_process
  parcel at the exact moment it dequeues, beside `dequeued_at`.
  `ready_for_next_task.bb` also gained the same
  `(when (= *file* (System/getProperty "babashka.file")) (-main))` guard
  `ready_for_next_batch.bb`'s sibling scripts already use elsewhere in
  this repo — without it, `load-file`-ing the script (needed to verify
  the stamp) fires the real claim loop against whatever mailbox state the
  loading process sits in.
- `swarm_handoff.bb`: `received_at_head` joins `reserved-fields` — no
  draft can forge it.
- `review_forward_evidence_gate_lib.bb`: new `received-at-head-for-task`,
  a sibling reader beside `received-commit-for-task` — same
  `received-parcel-for-task` lookup, so the commit and the head it reads
  always come from the identical parcel file. `nil` (never `""`) when
  absent (an older parcel, pre-BL-1610).
- `merge_drop_guard_lib.bb`:
  - `merge-commits` takes a `bound` (was `received`) - the scan is now
    `head..forwarded` when a stamp exists, falling back to
    `received..forwarded` otherwise (invariant 1).
  - Every finding gains `:excused` (invariant 2) —
    `excused-by-blob-identity?` is true only when the offending merge is
    NOT itself the forwarded commit (something, even nothing content-
    wise, comes after it) AND the forward's blob at the path matches what
    was received. Blob identity between received and forwarded ALONE
    over-excuses: BL-1576's own "taking the received side verbatim"
    scenario also leaves those blobs equal, but there the merge IS the
    forwarded tip — nothing superseded it, the drop rides this exact
    send. Discriminated by commit identity (`full-sha`), not a further
    blob change at the merge itself, since a real superseded drop can
    rest at the identical value the offending merge itself produced (two
    of BL-1606's six merges did).
  - `blocking-findings`/`blocked?`/`refusal-message` only act on
    non-excused findings.
  - `findings-for-git-handoff` reads the head stamp and passes it
    through.

## A mid-course correction (for the record)

My first `excused-by-blob-identity?` compared blob(merge-commit) against
blob(forwarded) to detect "something changed since this merge" - BL-1576's
own acceptance feature caught that this is wrong: its "taking the received
side verbatim" scenario resolves the merge to content that equals the
received blob AT THAT SAME MERGE (merge-commit === forwarded, no further
commit), which my first version wrongly excused. Re-running BL-1576's
suite against the pre-BL-1610 tree first confirmed 11/11 green there,
narrowing the regression to my own change before fixing it to compare
commit identity (`merge-commit != forwarded`) instead.
