## Spec observation — raised, adjudicated, adopted

Scenario 03 originally said the closing pass "observes fresh coordinator mail
and does not hibernate". In the code, fresh mail is not an input to the
DOWN-trigger (`should-hibernate?`) at all — it is the BL-310 UP-trigger: a
hibernated swarm with fresh mail relaunches. A not-yet-hibernated, drained, idle
swarm hibernates whether or not mail is fresh, today and before this parcel.

The ticket requires the closing-pass decision be **unaffected**, so I did not
change `should-hibernate?` to match the sentence — that would be the behaviour
change invariant 2 forbids. A priority-`00` note went to the specifier instead.

The specifier amended the ticket in `98454ae8c4` (feature narrative, scenario
03, invariant 2, the description's trap section and e2e step 5), agreeing the
spec was wrong at mint. This parcel now implements the amended scenario: the
handler drives the real `should-relaunch?` from the hibernated, drained state
where fresh mail is the only thing separating relaunch from staying down, and
asserts both directions. Acceptance re-run against the amended feature: **4/4**.
