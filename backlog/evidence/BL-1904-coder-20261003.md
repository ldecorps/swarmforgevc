## Change
- `parcel_line_lib.bb`:
  - `foreign-checkout? [root own-root]` (pure) compares canonical paths, so a subdirectory's
    toplevel and a `..`-spelled path both match. `own-root` nil is never foreign.
  - `move-decision` takes `:foreign-checkout?` and answers `{:action :foreign}` after the three
    stay cases and ahead of the dirty block. A foreign checkout that would stay stays silently;
    one that would move or block is refused.
  - `take-up!` takes an optional `:own-root`. The `:foreign` branch prints one line,
    `PARCEL_LINE: <root> is not <role>'s own worktree (<own-root>); the parcel was not taken up there.`,
    and writes no parcel-backup ref.
- `ready_for_next_task.bb` `take-up-parcel-line!` passes `:own-root (:worktree-path role-info)`,
  the role-info it already loads.
  - A role without a row never reaches `take-up!`: `parcel-intent` already skips
    `:unknown-role`.
  - The direct `take-up!` callers (bl1887 handler, `parcel_line_lib_test_runner.bb`, the
    bl1871/bl1887 property tests) pass no `:own-root`, so they are unchanged.
- Handler `specs/pipeline/steps/bl1904TakeUpMovesOnlyOwnWorktreeSteps.js`:
  - A `git init` with a bare origin under mkdtemp, proven by `--git-common-dir`, with coder,
    coder@2 and QA linked worktrees registered in its own roles.tsv.

    the chosen cwd with `SWARMFORGE_ROLE`.
