## The scan
TypeScript's parser (a declared dev dependency), loaded lazily, so a handler require costs 6 ms
(the module-load budget).
- **Real-dir values:** any binding whose initializer mentions `__dirname` or another real-dir
  binding, to a fixpoint.
- **Dispatcher paths:** a real-dir binding naming a dispatcher exactly
  (`ready_for_next_task.bb` never matches `ready_for_next_task.sh`); an inline `path.join` or
  `path.resolve` over a real-dir value and a dispatcher; and aliases of either, including
  `for...of` loop variables. Uses resolve to the innermost visible declaration, so a same-named
  variable in another function is never confused with one.
- **Judging a use:** each use goes to the innermost call that receives it, never climbing out of
  its statement or function.
  - `path.*` and `fs.realpathSync` pass their result on.
  - Read-only fs calls and assertions are safe.
  - Anything else flags: spawns, local wrappers, imported helpers, and unknown callees.

  handlers (bl1611, bl1642, bl1645, complianceBattery), and none is flagged.
