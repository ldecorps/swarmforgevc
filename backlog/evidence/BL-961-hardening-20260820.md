### Method note for the next hardener — do NOT read flat CPU as a stall here

A first attempt was **killed prematurely at ~4 minutes** on the documented BL-687
flat-CPU signature: `bb gherkin-mutator` and `mutationWorker.js` both sat at 0.0% CPU
with `TIME` advancing 0:00.10 -> 0:00.12, while the status line still read
`completed=0 running=0`. That reading was **wrong**. The mutant workspaces were being
generated the whole time — `tmp/<work-dir>/mutations/` grew m1..m4 -> m1..m5 across the
polls — and the relaunched run finished normally in 113s.

The reliable progress signal during the pre-run phase is the **mutant-directory count**
under the work dir (and, once running, `completed=`), not process CPU time: workspace
generation is I/O-bound and legitimately shows flat CPU. The status line also does not
re-print while `completed=0`, so an unchanging log is not evidence either. Raised to the
specifier as a `rule_proposal` so BL-687's discriminator gets this qualifier.
