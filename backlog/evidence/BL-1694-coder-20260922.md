## A bug I found and fixed in my own first draft

My first version of the new "no /clear sent to documenter" assertion used
`grep -c "..." "$CALL_LOG" | grep -q '^0$'` - under this file's own
`set -o pipefail`, `grep -c` exits 1 on a zero-count match even though it
still prints "0" to stdout, and `pipefail` propagates that as pipeline
failure regardless of the downstream `grep -q` succeeding. The assertion
looked like a hung/broken fake tmux (a real `/clear` reaching documenter)
until traced with a debug dump of the call log, which showed no such line
ever appeared. Fixed by capturing the count into a variable with
`|| true` instead of piping. Same trap is latent everywhere else a
zero-count grep -c feeds a pipe under pipefail in this codebase, though
no other line here does it.
