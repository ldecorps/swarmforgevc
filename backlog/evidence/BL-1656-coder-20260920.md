# BL-1656 coder evidence

## What was wrong

`extension/test/bl1368ApprovalCommitByline.property.test.js`'s third test
memoised a `{own, humanDecision}` verdict pair per role the property's
`fc.constantFrom(...PIPELINE_ORDER)` generator happened to draw across 60
runs, then asserted the memoised role set equalled `PIPELINE_ORDER` -
reach hoped for from a uniform random draw over seven roles, not built.
With ~1/7 chance of missing any given role per draw and 60 draws, roughly
1 run in 1500 misses at least one role. On 2026-09-20 the property lane
failed on the coder's BL-1630 worktree with exactly that shape
("specifier" never drawn); 3/3 green in isolation immediately after
confirmed no real defect.

## The fix

Per the ticket's own "How" (option 1): the verdict pair for every role in
`PIPELINE_ORDER` is now computed in a plain `for` loop BEFORE `fc.assert`
runs, so the reach assertion (`assert.deepEqual([...verdicts.keys()].sort(),
[...PIPELINE_ORDER].sort())`, itself unchanged) is true by construction on
every run, independent of what the random draw visits. The property body
is unchanged in meaning - it still reads `verdicts.get(role)` and checks
the same two invariants (a role's own commit passes; a human-decision
commit does not) - it just no longer has to populate the map itself.
Fixture cost is unchanged: exactly seven role pairs of git commits +
`compliance_battery.bb` spawns, same as before (the deterministic loop
IS the same seven calls the random draw used to make on its way to
covering all roles; `numRuns: 60` still runs, exercising the two
invariants redundantly over the now-guaranteed-populated map).

No change to `compliance_battery.bb`'s commit-byline check or to
`humanDecisionCommitMessage` (constraint honored). The other two tests in
this file, and their own reach assertions, are untouched.
