## Shape chosen: 1 (widen the detector), and why

The ticket offered two shapes and left the choice to the coder and architect.
The live `.feature` decides it: scenarios 01, 02 and 03 all end in **"the
sibling ticket is named in the report"**. Shape 2 (narrow the replay, refuse
the path) refuses rather than names, so it cannot satisfy the acceptance
contract the specifier already wrote. Shape 1 is implemented.

The change is one flag, in one place: `ancestry-commits` in
`swarmforge/scripts/land_step_lib.bb` drops `--first-parent`, so the
detector's candidate set becomes the full `origin/main..<tip>` ancestry — a
superset of every commit the replay's `:delivered` diff can draw content
from. `own-commit-changed-paths` and `task-tagged-changed-paths` are
deliberately **untouched**: the replay must still reproduce what the parcel
put on the branch (BL-1297).

Noise (the cost the ticket names, and BL-1272's territory): the widened walk
names more siblings, but a sibling whose content is already byte-identical on
`origin/main` still lands in `:landed`, and `entanglement-note` still names
only `:unlanded`. So the extra names arrive on the `LANDED_SIBLING` line, not
as new adjudication requests.
