## What changed, operator-visible
BL-658 (daemon overnight freeze/drain/briefing/stop) and BL-820 (lean
pass, `./finish-shift` only) were two mechanisms with two triggers — an
ordinary weekday 17:00 bedtime, the normal way this swarm sleeps, got the
lean pass alone and never ran the full ceremony. Per the human's directive
("820 should be part of 658 ... each time the swarm does at least 1 shift
and goes to sleep"), `night-closing-ceremony-run.ts` is now THE ceremony:
freeze, drain, lean pass, briefing/email, stop — one sequence. Every sleep
path (`finish-shift`, `night-stop.sh`, and callers like
`day-shift-bedtime.sh`/`wait_for_expedite_then_bedtime.sh`) now passes
`--sleep-path <name>` so the ceremony runs whatever the hour, unlike the
daemon's own trigger which stays gated by its overnight closure window. A
restart (`remote_bounce.sh`, hotfix relaunch, expedite park) never invokes
it. "Did at least one shift" reads existing mtimes
(`.swarmforge/shift-started`/`.swarmforge/swarm-identity` vs the newest
ceremony record) and fails OPEN (never silences on an unreadable stamp).
The shift schedule itself is untouched — this changes WHEN the ceremony
runs, never WHICH shifts exist.
