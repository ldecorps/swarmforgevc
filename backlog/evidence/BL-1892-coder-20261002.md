# BL-1892 coder pass (2026-10-02)

## Built - `swarmforge/scripts/land_main_publish.sh`
- `land_install_traps` (used by `run_land` and `run_push`): EXIT keeps `land_release_trap`; INT and
  TERM get `land_signal_trap`, which releases the lock and exits 143 at once (printing
  `LAND_STOPPED: signalled ...`) - never returns into the land as before.
- `land_repoint` (both re-point call sites): sets `LAND_IN_REPOINT`; a signal meanwhile only records
  `LAND_STOP_REQUESTED`, and the land releases and exits 143 the moment the re-point returns.
  Two shields, both needed (each found by the scenario 02 fixture failing without it):
  1. the re-point runs in its own process group (`perl -e 'setpgrp(0,0); exec @ARGV'`), so a TERM to
     the land's whole group cannot reach it. An inherited "ignore" is not enough: bb is a
     native-image JVM and installs its own SIGTERM handler. perl, not setsid(1), because macOS ships
     no setsid.
  2. the command-substitution subshell that waits for it ignores INT/TERM: with traps set, bash keeps
     that subshell (no exec optimisation) inside the land's group, and a group TERM killed it - the
     land then exited before the re-point finished.
- The EXIT path is unchanged; `test_bl1366_land_is_one_command.sh` 23/23 PASS.

## Invariant (BL-654) - `extension/test/bl1892TerminatedLandNeverPushesLockless.property.test.js`
16 draws: moment (pre-push hold / mid re-point / random delay) x signal (TERM, TERM, INT) x target
(land / whole group), real land each draw; hooks timestamp push and re-point starts, the driver
timestamps the signal. When the land honours the signal, nothing starts after it; otherwise it ran
to its normal end. Lock gone every draw. Reach floors: every moment and target >= 2, pre-push >= 2,
mid re-point >= 2, honoured stops >= 5. Fails with origin/main's script (checked); ~70 s.
Finding while writing it: a SIGINT to the land alone while it waits on a child that exits normally
runs no trap at all (bash's wait-and-cooperative-exit rule), so the land finishes with its lock held -
correct under the invariant, and why the oracle keys on LAND_STOPPED rather than on the signal.
