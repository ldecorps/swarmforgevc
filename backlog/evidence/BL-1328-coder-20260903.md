`swarmforge/scripts/swarmforge.sh` now matches a qwen model in BOTH token

credential/endpoint mismatch, not just a missing context declaration), and
that no live pack combines the two today. Neither precedence was changed —
the human's BL-1324 ruling asked to document the asymmetry, not resolve it.
