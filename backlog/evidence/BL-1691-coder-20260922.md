## Two pre-existing generator/classifier defects found while constructing bl1210

`idKind()`'s own classification disagrees with three members of the
generator pools the ORIGINAL combined-sampling test already drew from
(masked because that approach bucketed by `idKind()`'s own verdict
post-hoc, never by which named generator produced the value):
`EPIC_IDS()`'s `'bubble'` has no dash (`idKind` falls through to
`'role'`), `STANDING_IDS()`'s `'Approvals'` is not all-uppercase (falls
through to `'role'`), and `ROLE_IDS()`'s `'QA'` IS all-uppercase
(`idKind` reads it as `'standing'` before ever reaching the role
fallback). Construction surfaced this immediately (a strict per-draw
`assert.equal(idKind(id), kind, ...)` failed on the first run). Fixed by
filtering each cell's own generator to only the members `idKind()`
actually classifies as that kind (`forKind(kind, generatorFn)`) - the
literal pools (`EPIC_IDS`, `STANDING_IDS`, `ROLE_IDS`) and `idKind()`
itself are both untouched; this is a coder-domain test fixture fix, not
a production finding, so no separate note was filed.
