## What changed, operator-visible
`land_main_publish.sh` documented the QA land protocol in its own header
(acquire lock, decide, rematch if advised, push FF-only, release) but
implemented no mode that performed it — QA hand-ran all four steps plus
`land_step_cli.bb` and `issue_done.sh` after every approval. A new `--land
<task-name> <approved-commit> [<issue-ref>]` mode performs the whole
sequence as one command, branching on `land_step_cli.bb`'s
`LAND_CLEAN`/`LAND_REPLAY`/`LAND_ESCALATE` verdict (escalation is never
auto-resolved — reported as `LAND_STOPPED`), with a bounded-deadline lock
(`LAND_LOCK_TIMEOUT`) released on every exit path via a trap, at most one
rematch (`LAND_REMATCH`) before waiting on the lock, and no `--force`
anywhere on the push path.
