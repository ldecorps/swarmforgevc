which is why the test's own internal timeout was set to 120000ms (not
tighter) - margin against exactly this kind of transient contention on a
live, heavily-loaded multi-agent host, while staying far under the 180s
rerun ceiling that actually gates commits.

## Invariants (BL-654) — stated reason, no property test (both)

Both declared invariants are about this EXISTING property file's own
reach-math shape (floors derived from a constant, never dropped; no
property deleted or weakened) - not a new pure module a `test:properties`
generator could drive. They are deterministic-by-construction claims
(the ticket's own words: "the probability of a floor failing... is ZERO,
not a small number"), discharged directly by the acceptance scenarios
above running the REAL file and asserting its REAL coverage output and
REAL guard verdicts - the same posture BL-1349's own evidence used for
its sibling tuning ticket, and the established pattern for a change to an
existing spawn-heavy property file's own tuning constants rather than a
new production module.
