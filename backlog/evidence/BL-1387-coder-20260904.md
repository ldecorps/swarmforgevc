# BL-1387 — CODER PASS, 2026-09-04

The detector half of the 2026-09-04 poisoned merge. BL-1386 (handed to the
cleaner earlier this session) stops the daemon orphaning its own merge; this
one stops every open merge being read as a human's.

## The defect

`absorb-dispatch-plan` and its siblings mapped `merge-head-present?` straight
to `:skip-human-merge-in-progress`, and the daemon's predicate was
`git rev-parse --verify MERGE_HEAD`. That is presence, not ownership — it
cannot tell a human, another agent, and the daemon's own previous tick apart.
On 2026-09-04 it held two roles and the daemon for fifteen minutes on a merge
with no owning process whose index carried NONE of the incoming side. A
`git commit` there would have written a merge whose tree reverted the whole
origin side.

## What changed

**`master_main_reconcile_lib.bb`** (pure):

- `classify-open-merge` → `:none | :own | :live-human | :orphaned`. The
  asymmetry is the point: `:live-human` needs POSITIVE evidence (a git process
  on this checkout, or a fresh lock), because that is the reading that makes
  every role hold; `:orphaned` is what is left when nothing vouches for the
  merge. Presence alone can never yield `:live-human` again (invariant 1).
- `index-lock-fresh?` with a 60s window. An absent lock, an unreadable mtime,
  or an uncomparable clock is NOT freshness — absence of evidence never
  manufactures an owner.
- `index-carries-incoming?` — the intersection of `git diff --cached
  --name-only HEAD` with `git diff --name-only HEAD MERGE_HEAD`. Returns
  **nil** when either side is unreadable rather than a fabricated `false`, so a
  caller says "could not tell" instead of asserting a poisoning it did not
  observe (invariant 3).
- `open-merge-branch` — ONE mapping from an open merge to a plan branch,
  shared by every plan that used to test presence directly so the three cannot
  drift. `merge-class` is optional: absent, every caller behaves EXACTLY as
  before this ticket, which is what keeps the existing suites and every other
  call site unchanged.
- `:orphaned-merge` in `surface-message`, plus `orphaned-merge-escalation`.

**`handoffd.bb`** (the side-effecting edits): liveness via **`pgrep -x git`
plus `/proc/<pid>/cwd`** — never `pgrep -f`, which self-matches this very
process (the ticket warns of it; I hit exactly that trap myself earlier
today) — the lock's real mtime, the index intersection, and the
`orphaned-merge` branch.

## A second site the ticket did not name

`master-main-rematch-onto-origin!` made the same presence-only assertion
independently of `absorb-dispatch-plan`. Invariant 1 says the reading is never
valid from presence alone, not "never valid in one function", and that site is
a RESET path — mistaking an orphan for a human there is what keeps a poisoned
merge alive rather than surfaced. Both are classified now; there is no
presence-only "this is a human's" assertion left in the daemon.

## The 80-char cap would have eaten the deliverable

The full remedy text is ~124 characters, so `surface-message`'s existing cap
fallback dropped BOTH facts the ticket requires — the sha and the index state
— leaving a bare "orphaned merge on master - abort and redo". Split rather
than truncated: the capped NOTE carries the two facts the clearer acts on
(which merge, and whether its index is safe), and the uncapped ESCALATION
carries the remedy plus its dangerous step — `git merge --abort` deletes
staged-new files, which is why the 2026-09-04 hand-clear had to back one up
first. A property sweeps sha lengths to prove the facts survive the cap
instead of being truncated away.

### Generator reach, by construction

Invariant 1 is about a CONJUNCTION of absences — no process AND no fresh lock
AND no record — so the whole 2×2×2 owner-signal cube is enumerated with reach
asserted, not drawn. An independent draw would visit the all-absent corner
about an eighth of the time, and that corner is the entire defect.

### Non-vacuity

The pre-BL-1387 predicate (presence ⇒ human) is reconstructed in the runner
and must be rejected by invariant 1. It is.

### The measurement that makes invariant 3 concrete

The fixture reports `unmergedPaths: 0` in EVERY shape, including the poisoned
one. That is the reading everyone reaches for, and it cannot distinguish a
safe index from one that would revert the origin side on commit.
