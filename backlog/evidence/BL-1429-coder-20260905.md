## Declared invariants (BL-654)

**Invariant 1** ("The register is a signal, never a raise... null only
when both are null, and Article 3.5's two named caps (1 and 0) are the
only values it ever recommends.") — property: for all 12 combinations of
{no/degraded/severe rework signal} × {no/count/age/unowned standing-red
signal}, `computeThrottleRecommendation`'s `recommendedCap` equals the
independently-computed fold of the two, and is always `null`, `0`, or `1`.
Reachability asserted directly: a `Set` of `${reworkCat}:${standingCat}`
pairs is checked to have all 12 members after the run (`numRuns: 60`).

**Invariant 2** ("Every change of the recommended cap is logged with the
signal that caused or cleared it (count, age or unowned)... unchanged
logs nothing.") — property: over random sequences (length 2-5) of
standing-red-only state transitions (no rework signal, isolating exactly
the three named signals the invariant's own parenthetical calls out), a
cap change writes EXACTLY one new log line naming the correct signal (and
provably not either of the other two signals' phrases), and an unchanged
cap writes none.

**Invariant 3** ("Thresholds are read from swarmforge.conf... with
defaults 10 and 7 when absent; an unowned red throttles at any
threshold.") — two properties: (a) thresholds round-trip through
swarmforge.conf exactly, independently per key (a present key is honored,
an absent key defaults), over random values and random presence/absence;
(b) `standingRedSignal`'s crossing logic is evaluated against the GIVEN
thresholds (never a hardcoded 10/7) across random threshold/count/age/
unowned-length combinations, and an unowned red recommends cap 1
regardless of how permissive the thresholds are (`numRuns: 300`, pure and
cheap).

### Non-vacuity — each mutation run for real and restored byte-identical

  empty.
- **Invariant 2**: the `if (priorCap !== recommendation.recommendedCap)`
  log-gate replaced with `if (true)` (log every call unconditionally).
  Failed immediately: "expected no new log line when the cap stayed null,
  got 1". Restored; diff empty.
- **Invariant 3**: `standingRedSignal`'s two comparisons hardcoded to
  literal `10`/`7`, ignoring the `thresholds` parameter. Failed
  immediately on the parametric property: a random `maxCount` below the
  crossed count produced `{recommendedCap:1,signal:'count'}` where the
  (correctly threshold-honoring) expectation was `null`. Restored; diff
  empty.
- All three restorations reconfirmed by recompiling and re-running the
  full property file plus all 35 unit/fixture tests (11 + 8 + 16) green.
